# 📱 Intern Us Feature Voting - Executive Brief

## 🎯 Purpose
Vote on 75 features for the Intern Us platform to prioritize our development roadmap.

---

## ⏱️ Time Required
- **Quick voting:** 10-15 minutes
- **Thoughtful review:** 20-30 minutes

---

## 📱 How to Use (Simple!)

### 1. **Open Link on Your Phone**
   - Best experience on mobile
   - Works on iPhone, Android, tablets

### 2. **Accept Privacy Terms**
   - First screen - just click "Accept"

### 3. **Enter Your Name**
   - Complete 3-slide onboarding
   - Enter your name at the end
   - Click "Start Exploring"

### 4. **Vote on Features**
   
   ```
   👈 Swipe LEFT          👉 Swipe RIGHT
      = NO (skip)            = YES (vote for it)
   ```

   **Or use buttons:**
   - ✗ = NO
   - ❤️ = YES

### 5. **Get Details (Optional)**
   - Tap "Learn More" on any card
   - Read full description
   - Ask AI assistant questions
   - Vote from detail view
